Add zooms

See documentation (opens in your browser)

Adds zoom cuts at specific intervals to emphasize certain words, based on what is being spoken.

For best results:
  • Ensure your audio does not have repetitions / multiple takes
  • Ensure your audio is clear and loud
  • Mute any tracks with music / sound effects

Zooms trigger

?
Choose when to add zooms:

Key phrases (default): Zoom in when speaker is saying something important.

Every other cut: Zoom into every other clip (specify which video track to analyze).

Every other sentence: Zoom into every other sentence being spoken.
🧪 Beta
Guidance video track
?
Specify which track to analyze to find clips. A zoom will be applied at every other clip on this track.
